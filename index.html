01. Understanding the Problem

Recorded videos contain valuable information, but retrieving a specific event still requires manually reviewing large amounts of footage. The challenge is to make recorded videos searchable using natural language descriptions.

Recorded Video
Natural Language Description
Semantic Understanding
Relevant Video Clips
Example Query
Find the person wearing a blue shirt who entered the shop around 4 PM.
Current Challenges
Hours of recorded footage to inspect manually.
No semantic understanding of video content.
Difficult to locate people, objects and events.
Evidence retrieval is slow and repetitive.
Timeline navigation depends entirely on humans.
Existing players only support timestamp-based search.
Goal

Instead of manually watching videos, users should simply describe what they are looking for and instantly jump to the relevant timestamp.
